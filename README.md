**Amazon Sales Data Visualization**

**Project Overview**

This project performs Data Visualization on the Amazon Sales Dataset to analyze product pricing, discounts, ratings, customer reviews, and category distribution. Various visualization techniques were used to identify trends, patterns, and insights from the data.

**Tasks Performed**


-Data Loading
-Data Cleaning
-Missing Value Handling
-Data Type Conversion
-Price Analysis
-Rating Analysis
-Discount Analysis
-Correlation Analysis
-Data Visualization


**Tools Used**


-Python
-Pandas
-NumPy
-Matplotlib
-Seaborn
-Jupyter Notebook


**Visualizations Created**


-Top 10 Most Reviewed Products
-Product Ratings Distribution
-Top Product Categories
-Discount Percentage Distribution
-Products with Highest Savings
-Correlation Heatmap


**Key Findings**


-Most products have ratings above 4.0, indicating high customer satisfaction.
-Electronics and accessories are the most common product categories.
-Discounts are widely used as a marketing strategy across products.
-Popular products receive significantly higher numbers of customer reviews.
-Products with higher actual prices generally have higher discounted prices.
-Customer ratings are only weakly influenced by discount percentages.

Amazon Sales Dataset
